# Proposal: Homepage Design Refactor

## Why

The current version of the homepage (`index.html`) is a simplified reconstruction that lacks many of the key design elements and sections of the original `https://grupoalfa.net` website. To provide a high-fidelity experience, the homepage needs to be refactored to achieve total visual parity, ensuring the layout, typography, and content match the original site exactly as seen in the provided screenshots and live site.

## What Changes

- **Structural Refactor**: Update `index.html` to include all missing sections (Process, Detailed Services, Submarcas buttons, Google Maps integration).
- **Styling Updates**: Refactor `home.css` (or equivalent) to implement the specific aesthetics of the original site, including the white/geometric hero background, orange-header service list, and minimalist typography.
- **Content Alignment**: Update text and images to match the original's hierarchy and messaging.
- **Asset Integration**: Ensure the correct high-quality assets (logos, icons) are used and localized.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `index-design-refactor`: Standardizes the layout and styling templates for the homepage to ensure consistent high-fidelity design.

## Impact

- **Affected Files**: `NewWeb/index.html`, `NewWeb/src/home.css`, and potentially `NewWeb/style.css`.
- **User Experience**: Signficantly improved visual quality and brand consistency, matching the "premium" feel of the original site.

## Why

Hero images are perceived as too dark and the client does not accept the page-name label in the lower-left corner. Adjusting this is necessary to align the visual presentation with client feedback.

## What Changes

- Lighten the visual treatment of Hero images across the site by reducing the dark overlay; specifically on `/index` the overlay transparency should be zero.
- Remove the lower-left page-name label from the Hero.
- Keep the remaining Hero behavior and content unchanged.

### New Capabilities

<!-- None. -->

### Modified Capabilities

- `hero-component`: Update presentation requirements to reduce image darkening and remove the lower-left label.

## Impact

Changes to styles and/or properties of the Hero component and the templates/pages that use it.

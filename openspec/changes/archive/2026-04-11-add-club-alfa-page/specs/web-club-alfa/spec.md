## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Club Alfa page availability
The system SHALL provide a dedicated `Club Alfa` page at `/club` as a first-level commercial destination within the Grupo Alfa site.

#### Scenario: User opens Club page
- **WHEN** a user navigates to `/club`
- **THEN** the site displays a dedicated Club Alfa page within the shared site layout.

### Requirement: Club Alfa commercial positioning
The Club Alfa page SHALL present Club Alfa as a private investor club using epic, community-driven messaging and a structured explanation of its value.

#### Scenario: User reads the hero and introduction
- **WHEN** the Club Alfa page loads
- **THEN** the page presents Club Alfa branding and messaging centered on membership, community identity, and access to Grupo Alfa opportunities.

### Requirement: Club Alfa benefits and coverage
The Club Alfa page SHALL explain the concrete member benefits, including ongoing accompaniment, monthly seminars, exclusive access, priority opportunities, and the investment areas covered by the club.

#### Scenario: User reviews what membership includes
- **WHEN** a user scrolls through the main content sections
- **THEN** the page presents the membership benefits and the covered areas of accompaniment in a structured format.

### Requirement: Club Alfa pricing and conversion
The Club Alfa page SHALL display the annual fee as `99€/Año` in Spanish and `99€/Year` in English, and include visible calls to action for joining or requesting more information.

#### Scenario: User reaches pricing and CTA sections
- **WHEN** a user reaches the pricing or closing sections of the page
- **THEN** the annual fee is visible and at least one CTA is available to continue toward contact or signup intent.

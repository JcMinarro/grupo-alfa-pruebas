## Why

Grupo Alfa is already advertising Club Alfa as a primary 99 EUR per year offer, but the current website does not provide a dedicated page or a clear navigation entry to explain it and capture demand. This creates a mismatch between the offline commercial promise and the digital experience at a time of immediate campaign urgency.

## What Changes

- Create a new `Club Alfa` commercial page with its value proposition, member benefits, accompaniment areas, monthly education, annual fee, and clear calls to action.
- Add global visibility for `Club` in the main site navigation as a first-level entry point.
- Reinforce the homepage so it presents Club Alfa as a prominent offer and routes traffic to the new page.
- Preserve the epic commercial tone requested by the client while removing ambiguous or unconfirmed promises.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `web-club-alfa`: Covers the new `Club Alfa` page, including its content structure, commercial messaging, benefits, and conversion CTAs.

### Modified Capabilities
- `web-standard-header`: The primary navigation changes to include `Club` as a first-level link on desktop and mobile.
- `web-index`: The homepage changes to give Club Alfa more prominence and link explicitly to the new page.

## Impact

- Page files in `src/pages/` for the new `Club` experience.
- Shared navigation in `src/components/Header.astro`.
- Translatable content in `src/i18n/ui.ts`.
- Possible link and CTA adjustments in the homepage and other acquisition entry points.

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Club Alfa pricing and conversion
The Club Alfa page SHALL display the annual fee as `99€/Año` in Spanish and `99€/Year` in English, and include visible calls to action that enter the public paid membership/campaign flow before account creation and payment.

#### Scenario: User reaches pricing and CTA sections
- **WHEN** a user reaches the pricing or closing sections of the page
- **THEN** the annual fee is visible and at least one primary CTA is available to continue toward paid membership review before sign-up rather than the generic contact flow
